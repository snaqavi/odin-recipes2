Description

This is a basic lasagna recipe explainig the steps to put a lasagna together once you have bought a majority of the ingredients

Ingredients
  • Pasta lasagna sauce
  • Pasta sheets
  • Mince meat or Meat ragu
  • Cheese
Recipe
  1. Add a thin layer of meat ragu onto the bottom of a baking tray
  2. Add a layer of pasta sheets on top of the meat
  3. Spread a layer of pasta sauce on top the pasta sheets
  4. Repeat this between 3-4 times
  5. On the final layer add a layer of cheese
  6. Place the baking dish with your lasagna into a pre-heated oven at 180 degrees for 20-30 minutes until the top is golden brown
  7. Remove the dish from the oven and enjoy your lasagna
